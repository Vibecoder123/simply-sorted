Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning collects through this website and when we work with you, what we do with it, and the choices you have. It sits alongside our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who we are

Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16997419). Our registered office is Waterloo House, 2 Colchester Road, Clacton-On-Sea, Essex, CO16 8HA, and we work from Basingstoke, Hampshire, trading as Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning. We decide how and why your information is used, which makes us the data controller under UK data protection law.

You can reach us at info@simplysortedspecialistcleaning.co.uk or on 07852 734 735.

2. What we collect

When you use the contact form or the quote form on this site, we receive:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number (optional on the contact form, required on the quote form)
  • the service, property type, property size and preferred date you choose on the quote form
  • anything you type into the message or notes box

If you book a job with us, we also hold the address of the property and any access arrangements you give us, such as how we will get in.

Our cleaners take before and after photos of the property. We share these with our team, including our social media manager. We do not post identifiable images without your approval, and you can withdraw that approval at any time.

Key safe, alarm and door codes are shared only between the owner and the cleaner assigned to your property.

3. Health and special category information

When you engage Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning for specialist services (such as bereavement cleaning, trauma cleaning, or hoarding clearance), you may choose to share details regarding your health, medical history, mental health, or personal circumstances.

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), information concerning physical or mental health is classified as "Special Category Data" and requires higher levels of protection.

Our Lawful Basis and Condition for Processing

We only collect and process this sensitive data with your explicit consent (pursuant to Article 9(2)(a) of the UK GDPR). When you fill out our inquiry forms, speak with our customer service team, or contract our services, we will ask you to confirm your explicit agreement for us to record and use these details solely for the purpose of planning, safely executing, and tailoring our specialist cleaning services to your needs.

How We Protect Your Data

  • Strict Confidentiality: Access to your special category data is strictly limited to trained management and operational staff who require the information to perform the cleaning service safely.
  • Secure Storage: All notes and communications containing health or vulnerability details are shared through encrypted channels and managed securely.
  • Retention: We retain this data only as long as necessary to complete your service and fulfill legal/tax obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Right to Withdraw Consent

You have the right to withdraw your explicit consent to our processing of your special category data at any time. Please note that because these details are often essential for our team to safely and effectively carry out specialist cleans (such as hoarding or biohazard remediation), withdrawing consent may impact our ability to deliver or complete the service. To withdraw consent, please reach out at info@simplysortedspecialistcleaning.co.uk.

4. Why we use your information

We use your information for these reasons:

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote, because you asked us to.
  • To carry out the work, take payment and keep a record of the job, because we have an agreement with you.
  • To keep the financial and business records the law requires.
  • To deal with complaints and damage claims, and to protect our business, where that is fair to you.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of services that handle it on our behalf:

  • Formspree receives what you type into our contact and quote forms and passes it to us.
  • DocFlite holds our customer records and job forms.
  • WhatsApp carries messages and job photos between our team, including our social media manager.

We may also share information where the law requires us to.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. The contact and quote pages load a form script from unpkg.com, a file delivery service, which can see your IP address in the same way any website you visit can.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for 12 months after you become inactive, then delete it. You become inactive when you stop being a customer, or when we have not carried out work for you for 12 weeks. Enquiries that do not lead to a job are deleted within 30 days. Job photos are kept for 90 days, apart from any you have approved for posting, which stay on our social media until you withdraw your approval. We review what we hold every quarter.

We keep invoices and payment records for six years, as the law requires for limited companies.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, and object to us using it. In some cases you can also ask for it in a form you can pass to someone else. Where we rely on your consent, for example to use photos, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use any of these rights, email us at info@simplysortedspecialistcleaning.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the way we handle information changes.
