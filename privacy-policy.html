Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning collects through this website and when we work with you, what we do with it, and the choices you have. It sits alongside our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who we are

Simply Sorted Specialist Cleaning is a cleaning business based in Basingstoke, Hampshire. We decide how and why your information is used, which makes us the data controller under UK data protection law.

For Kirsty: business details

The legal wording needs the exact details of who is responsible for your customers' information.

  • Are you a sole trader or a limited company? What is the exact name you trade under, and the company number if you have one?
  • What address should appear on the policy? A business address is fine, it does not have to be your home.
  • Have you registered with the ICO (the Information Commissioner's Office) and paid the data protection fee? If yes, what is your registration number? If you were told you are exempt, who told you?

You can reach us at info@simplysortedspecialistcleaning.co.uk or on 07852 734 735.

2. What we collect

When you use the contact form or the quote form on this site, we receive:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number (optional on the contact form, required on the quote form)
  • the service, property type, property size and preferred date you choose on the quote form
  • anything you type into the message or notes box

If you book a job with us, we also hold the address of the property and any access arrangements you give us, such as how we will get in.

For Kirsty: photos and access notes

Both change what this section has to say.

  • Do you take photos or videos of a property before, during or after a job? Are they ever kept after the job is finished?
  • Do you write down key safe codes, alarm codes or door codes anywhere? If so, where, and when do you delete them?
  • Apart from name, address, phone, email and payment, do you keep any other notes about customers?

3. If you tell us about a difficult situation

Some of our work follows a bereavement, a health problem or a difficult living situation. You do not have to explain why you need us, and we only need enough detail to plan the work safely. If you choose to tell us, through a form, a call or a message, we use it to give you a quote, plan the job and reply to you, and for nothing else. If you would rather not put these details in a web form, call us instead.

For Kirsty: how you handle sensitive details

This section makes promises on your behalf, so it has to match what really happens.

  • When a customer tells you about a bereavement, a health problem or similar, who sees it? Is it only you, or does your team too?
  • Where do those details end up: your phone, WhatsApp, a notebook, DocFlite? How long do they stay there?
  • Are you comfortable with the wording "and for nothing else"?

4. Why we use your information

We use your information for these reasons:

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote, because you asked us to.
  • To carry out the work, take payment and keep a record of the job, because we have an agreement with you.
  • To keep the financial and business records the law requires.
  • To deal with complaints and damage claims, and to protect our business, where that is fair to you.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a small number of services that handle it on our behalf:

  • Formspree receives what you type into our contact and quote forms and passes it to us.
  • DocFlite holds our customer records and job forms.

We may also share information where the law requires us to.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. The contact and quote pages load a form script from unpkg.com, a file delivery service, which can see your IP address in the same way any website you visit can.

For Kirsty: nothing needed now

If visitor tracking is added later (it is a separate piece of work), this section will be rewritten and a cookie banner added.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the reasons in section 4.

For Kirsty: how long you keep things

A policy should give real periods, not a general promise.

  • How long do you keep enquiries that never turn into a job?
  • How long do you keep records of completed jobs? Your accountant can tell you the minimum for financial records.
  • How long do you keep photos, if you take any?

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, and object to us using it. In some cases you can also ask for it in a form you can pass to someone else. Where we rely on your consent, for example to use photos, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use any of these rights, email us at info@simplysortedspecialistcleaning.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

For Kirsty: who deals with requests
  • Who reads and answers requests like these? Is it you?
  • Is info@ the right address, or would you like a separate one?

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the way we handle information changes.
