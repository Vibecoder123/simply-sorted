More Than End of Tenancy

When an Empty Property Needs More Than a Standard Clean

An end of tenancy clean returns a lived-in home to the standard on the inventory. A void clean starts from wherever the property was left. Some are fairly straightforward. Others have been left in extremely poor condition, with abandoned furniture and belongings, rooms that haven't been cleaned in months, or hazards that need dealing with before anyone else can safely go in.

We take on both. We work with private landlords, letting agents, housing associations, councils, property managers and supported housing providers, on flats, houses, HMOs, supported accommodation and commercial properties.

If you're a tenant moving out and want your deposit back, our end of tenancy cleaning is the right service.

What's Included

What a Void Clean Can Cover

Every void property is assessed individually, and the quote covers the work that property needs. Depending on its condition, that can include:

  • Rubbish clearance and removal of abandoned furniture and belongings
  • A full top to bottom deep clean
  • Kitchen, bathroom, oven and appliance cleaning
  • Carpet cleaning, using our Texatherm low-moisture system or hot water extraction depending on the carpet and its condition
  • Wall washing
  • Internal and external window cleaning
  • Garden clearance
  • Cleaning and disinfection
  • Mould treatment
  • Needle sweeps and sharps removal
  • Biohazard cleaning, including bodily fluids and other contamination

We're a registered waste carrier, and the cost of waste disposal is included in your quote.

Common Questions

Void Property Cleaning: Your Questions

How quickly can you clean a void property?
We can usually complete a void clean within a few days of booking, depending on availability, the size of the property and its condition. If a job is urgent, tell us and we'll always try to fit it in.
Is biohazard cleaning included?
It isn't automatically part of every void clean, but it doesn't need booking separately either. If we find contamination during the assessment, the specialist cleaning is included in the overall quote.
How much does a void clean cost?
Every property is assessed individually. The quote is based on its condition, the work required, any specialist services needed and waste disposal, so you know the full cost before we start.
Do you take away the rubbish and furniture?
Yes. We clear abandoned furniture, belongings and rubbish, and as a registered waste carrier we arrange its disposal. The cost is included in your quote.
