About Simply Sorted

Cleaning You Can Actually Trust

We know inviting someone in to clean isn't always straightforward. Whether it's a busy workspace, a property that needs a full reset, or something more sensitive like a biohazard or trauma clean, we handle it professionally and without judgement.

Our team are fully insured, DBS checked, and arrive equipped with everything needed to get the job done properly. No cutting corners. No pressure on you to prepare beforehand. We tidy as we clean.

Based right here in Basingstoke, you deal with real people who take ownership of the work and stand by the results.
